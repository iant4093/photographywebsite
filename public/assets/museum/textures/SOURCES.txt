American Walnut Veneer — Joinery
Source: https://polyhaven.com/a/american_walnut_veneer
Author: Jenelle van Heerden / Poly Haven
License: CC0, https://polyhaven.com/license
Downloaded from the official Poly Haven API/CDN on 2026-09-04.
Files: joinery_wood_albedo_512.jpg, joinery_wood_normal_512.jpg,
       joinery_wood_roughness_512.jpg
Matching source maps resized together from 1024px to 512px and JPEG-compressed.
Continuous horizontal walnut grain, one-metre seamless source sample. This is
furniture veneer rather than flooring, so the joinery has no plank seams.
Normal maps use OpenGL (+Y) convention; normal/roughness are linear data.

Authored Surface Response Maps
Files: fine_plaster_normal_512.jpg, fine_plaster_roughness_512.png,
       brushed_brass_roughness_256.png, ceramic_roughness_256.png,
       fabric_weave_normal_256.png, fabric_weave_roughness_256.png
Created for this project on 2026-09-04 by the deterministic offline generator
scripts/materials/prepare_gallery_materials.py. Original procedural material
data, no photographs, generated artwork, or third-party source images used.
Periodic shallow plaster pores, satin metal brushing, ceramic glaze variation,
and soft woven textile response. No painted highlights or shadows. All these
maps are linear data; grayscale roughness decodes to equal R/G/B values.
Fine plaster retains the original 512px analytic normal field and is delivered
as quality-92 JPEG with 4:4:4 chroma sampling. This replaces the lossless PNG
delivery with a fresh URL and reduces transfer by 151,681 bytes. The generator
checks decoded channel error and tile seams against the lossless analytic data.
Other generated response maps remain lossless PNG files.

Reproduction and budgets
See scripts/materials/README.md and src/utils/museumMaterialAssets.js.
The preparation script pins the six downloaded source files by SHA-256, keeps
PBR maps aligned, and checks dimensions, color-space metadata, seams, transfer,
and conservative RGBA+mipmap memory. Poly Haven source width: wood floor 1.7m,
walnut veneer 1m.
